**Formal review trigger:** after two analogous reds, every five accepted gates, each milestone transition, any new counterexample, or the user's request for the big picture. Reassess dependencies, prune redundant work, retain failures, keep explicit blockers. Never promise a spectral result from an algebraic or Hilbert-space result.
