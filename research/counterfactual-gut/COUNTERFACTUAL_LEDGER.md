## Claim classification

- **MATH-REFERENCE:** established abstract mathematics, not source-qualified here.
- **SOURCE-REFERENCE:** declaration exists in our exact source, not necessarily independently requalified in this branch.
- **CONDITIONAL:** logically motivated implication only under explicit premises.
- **OPEN:** not yet demonstrated on original BFSS carriers.
- **OBSTRUCTION:** identified mismatch or missing necessary structure; may rule out a narrow route, *not* the complete theory.
- **REJECTED:** a clearly stated implication is invalid; retain with counterexample/argument.
- **QUALIFIED:** reserved for exact native+independent proof or empirically supported result, with precise scope/evidence.

## Research decision gates (in order of falsification value)

**D0 — Mathematical vs physical topology.** Precisely identify domain/codomain of the Hopf map and what it means under all original BFSS gauge operations, boundary conditions and dynamics. Stop the particle route if only internal-label topology is available.

**D1 — Original Fock coupling.** Identify a densely defined, source-exact fermionic operator on the actual gauge-physical carrier coupled to a hypothesized hopfion background. Establish basic self-adjoint/Fredholm issues. A finite-mode CAR matrix alone does not provide a spacetime Dirac index.

**D2 — Chirality test.** Prove or refute robust spectral flow / unpaired fermionic zero modes in a legitimate effective spacetime theory. Record if modes pair, dissolve in continuum, or lose protection.

**D3 — 4D gauge and generation accounting.** Construct actual relativistic local EFT and enumerate representations under (SU(3)_C\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y). Do not insert 3 generations by hand while claiming derivation. Test anomaly and electroweak consistency.

**D4 — Unification discrimination.** Compare at least: no-GUT SM EFT, traditional GUT embeddings, emerging internal symmetry, and no-hopfion-matter null model. Compute what each predicts that others do not; check current experiments.
