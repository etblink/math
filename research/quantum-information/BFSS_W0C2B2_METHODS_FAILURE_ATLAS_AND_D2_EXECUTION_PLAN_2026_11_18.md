## 4. Root-cause synthesis

The repeated **common denominator** in B2 R1/R2, D0 R2, and D1 R1 is *computational elaboration at the concrete-original-source conversion boundary*, not a demonstrated counterexample to Lie bracket closure. Generic laws of subtraction, Jacobi and coefficient covariance are already qualified. D1's failed `rfl` showed that **renaming huge expressions does not hide them**. The source `unfold` technique in qualified W0C2A is stronger evidence than an untested new `rfl`/additive-map shortcut. We must distinguish source-level theorem construction, independent consumer compatibility, and heavy dependent cold build budget.
