## 4. Proposed hypothesis-transfer worksheet and DAG

For each candidate paper record the following **before** formalization:

1. **Bibliography and exact source register ID:** DOI/arXiv version, original theorem number/page and quoted assumptions; distinguish report metadata from text actually read.
2. **Transferable mathematical content:** self-contained precise proposition; whether source statement is theorem, conjecture, heuristic, numerical result or counterexample.
3. **Hypothesis audit:** map source assumptions individually to original BFSS data; mark absent assumptions as **OPEN**, not silently replaced.
4. **Target interface:** fully qualified original Lean definitions for FullL2, SmoothCore, source representations, gauge projection, coreForm and original closed-form domain.
5. **Weaker-framework/countermodel test:** can an elementary finite-dimensional or generic argument yield the same result without the source's preferred physics? Does a known BFSS valley escape state defeat it?
6. **Proof-DAG nodes:** isolate each assumption bridge as an independent lemma; then propose the composite theorem and independent consumer.
7. **Status and ceiling:** a registered reference is not a qualified transferable proof, a proof is not empirical selection, and a negative transfer is still preserved.

The source corpus stays frozen. A separate, explicit future FCP operation would be needed to change canonical source bindings, comparisons or claim ledgers. Routine extraction into the **math research branch** does not modify them.
