# IBI promotion and stopping rule — Project Lead proposal

Date: 2026-11-15 Pacific. Branch:
`research/bfss-incidence-unified-20261114`.
**Status: proposed governance, NOT owner-adopted merge authorization.**
Neither `integration/bfss-unified-research-20261108`, `main`, nor
the original incidence pilot has been modified by this proposal.

This rule complements, and does not supersede, the accepted
[unified-integration evidence policy](../spectral-p2-original-field/INTEGRATION_GATE_POLICY.md)
and [scientific method](../methodology/BFSS_SCIENTIFIC_METHOD_PROTOCOL.md).

## Two independent verdicts

**MATH-PROMOTABLE (bounded theorem bundle):** A coherent collection
of new original-source Lean declarations is P3 admissible only if
it is source-exact; every imported experimental dependency and
independent consumer is fully qualified at exact immutable blobs;
strict cold source/consumer builds pass with standard-only axioms;
there is no unsound assumption, redundant redefinition, wrong signed
normalization, or known source counterexample; and at least one
**nontrivial existing BFSS proof or independent source consumer**
actually uses the bundle in an identified proof obligation. The
bundle can then be considered for clean, bounded promotion, with its
claim ceiling attached. A green for only G0A or only the generic
quaternion polynomial G0B0 is mathematically accepted **but not
yet an independently justified full research-branch integration**.

**IBI-STRATEGY-ADOPTABLE (physical research allocation):** In
addition, the reconstructed incidence data must provide a validated
advantage in the *literal* BFSS physical Hilbert/Fock/gauge or
operator-domain theory: e.g. a previously unavailable exact
physical-state witness, gauge-equivariant domain/kinetic identity,
or a new controlled bound/obstruction that removes a named
original-source Hardy/charge/sector obligation. State the null
route and compare theorem assumptions, proof complexity and
qualification cost. The result need not prove the whole fidelity
certificate but must measurably change a scientific decision.

These predicates deliberately do **not** establish that incidence
is ontologically prior, that BFSS is an effective theory, a ToE,
a mass gap, original ground-state existence or Standard Model families.
Those would need new independent physical/evidential criteria.

## Finite classical source reconstruction exit package

1. **Already qualified:** G0A original `Boson 2` Gram invariance,
   strict #43689689837; G0B0 generic quaternion determinant identity,
   strict #43692012496. The latter is *not* an original-source
   determinant-one theorem. Preserve G0B0 R1 red.
2. **G1A**: original-color Lie-triple invariance
   `L_ijk = <colorLie(x_i,x_j),x_k>`, currently prospective strict
   #43693100174 at tested `42944aec`. Every proof requires a
   separate source-typed independent consumer.
3. **G1B**: signed nonzero original normalized Pauli/epsilon
   equality `L_ijk=c D_ijk`; exhibit a literal source full-rank
   orientation-reversal counterexample to Gram-only separation.
4. **G0B1/G0C** (or a separately pre-registered *equally strong
   exact-source alternative*): original G3A determinant +1 and
   source image exactly SO(3), not an abstract unlinked cover.
5. **G2**: source-exact rank-split statement
   `same G and signed D ↔ same original SU(2) bosonic orbit`,
   including low-rank cases and first-order rank-two control,
   with a genuine independent consumer. Distinguish orbit-point
   separation from polynomial invariant-ring generation, smooth
   quotient atlas and groupoid/Fock descent.

Completion of 1–5 establishes a **classical orbit reconstruction
package** for promotion consideration, not an original quantum
quotient or ontology. A smaller mathematically complete source
bundle may be eligible earlier if a concrete downstream mainline
consumer makes it genuinely reusable.

## Nontrivial consumer and null control

The conventional SO(3) model gives a decisive negative control:
Gram-only coordinates fold rank-two transverse directions
`z↔-z`, while selected oriented triples recover all seven; an
orientation-odd scalar Schwartz function
`D_012(X) exp(-||X||²)` is SO(3)-invariant, nonzero L², and
cannot depend on G alone. These are preserved *conventional*
results, NOT yet literal original gauge/Fock source-qualified
results.

A useful source transfer would prove a literal signed-triple
witness with source G2A and an independently checked BFSS theorem
that cannot be recovered from G alone. To make the **physical**
case, exhibit an exact source Fock gauge-invariant state/domain or
gauge-covariant kinetic/supercharge application using the new
invariants. The Fock fiber, source center action, measure and
domain assumptions must be explicit. A scalar toy calculation is
not a physical-state certificate.

## Hard-stop and bounded-review rules

- **Immediate STOP/revision** for an exact source counterexample
  to the proposed SO3-image, original orientation sign, complete
  orbit separation or claimed physical-state transfer. Preserve
  the counterexample and withdraw only the falsified statement.
- **Two analogous first-causal Lean reds**, the same typed
  source-conversion boundary, or a failed physics/source
  equivalence audit ⇒ pause that gate and change proof
  architecture; no unchanged CI retry or unbounded limits.
  A Lean red is `RED_UNQUALIFIED`, not mathematical falsification.
- **At most one more bounded classical reconstruction stage
  after the current G1A/G1B/G0B1/G0C/G2 package** without a
  concrete source-Fock/physical-domain consumer. If the package
  closes but no downstream payoff is found, PARK the ontology
  hypothesis and preserve mathematics/negatives as an auxiliary
  research track; do not continue adding attractive geometry
  indefinitely.
- **Review after five accepted gates, a stage transition, or
  two analogous reds**, whichever comes first. Record which
  original mainline obstruction was reduced; green counts
  alone never override this condition.
- If IBI consumes increasing resources and cannot outperform
  the existing original BFSS gauge/Hardy path, restore its
  priority to exploratory, even when all classical lemmas are
  correct.

## Safe, explicit promotion procedure

Do **NOT** fast-forward unified to the entire IBI research HEAD
merely because IBI was branched from unified and is ahead. The
IBI HEAD contains notes, exploratory work, prospective Lean
sources/workflows and red receipts; green closure of two gates
does not qualify the entire tree.

For a candidate promotion:
1. Freeze an exact accepted bundle of source, independent consumer,
   relevant qualification workflow, accepted receipt, and any
   indispensable proven dependencies. List paths and blobs.
2. Refresh actual unified HEAD and inspect both branch histories.
   Build a clean promotion candidate **on current unified HEAD**
   containing only qualified reusable changes, with exploratory
   work and unqualified dependencies excluded. Preserve all
   historic IBI commits and red receipts on the original research
   branch; no history rewrite or forced update.
3. Require pinned strict cold source + independent consumer on the
   candidate exact SHA, allowed only `propext`,
   `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, no sorryAx.
   Verify source-specific nontrivial downstream use, compile
   import closure, control original source and normalizations, and
   audit full diff against unified. Existing green is useful
   evidence but does not automatically qualify changed bytes.
4. Present the precise scientific scope, exclusions and the exact
   candidate commit for **explicit owner approval**. Only then
   allow a non-force fast-forward compare-and-swap update of
   `integration/bfss-unified-research-20261108`, checking the old
   SHA hasn't moved. Never touch `main` or FCP by implication.

## Decision summary

- **Classical gates valid but not useful:** preserve and PARK,
  not merge entire ontology track.
- **Useful bounded new mathematics:** qualified P3 promotion
  candidate, independent of grander ontology.
- **Physically useful source bridge:** promote the qualified
  mathematics and formally reconsider IBI as a high-priority
  research method, not an established ontology.
- **Source counterexample:** label the exact claim FALSIFIED,
  preserve evidence and redirect, rather than accumulating more
  green auxiliary lemmas.
