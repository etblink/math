## Structural explanation of the nine eigenvalues

For a unit rank-one configuration, choose the color axis 2 and diagonalize its real symmetric spatial gamma matrix by an orthogonal spin-basis change. Gamma has eight +1 and eight -1 eigenvalues by the Clifford relations (any perpendicular unit gamma anticommutes and exchanges eigenspaces). Let P_j=i t_{j,0}t_{j,1} in the rotated Majorana basis. These sixteen Hermitian involutions commute. The scaled Yukawa Z is sum s_j P_j with eight signs of each kind; the gauge-plane generator is proportional to C=sum P_j. On C-neutral vectors the joint spectral sign pattern satisfies sum p_j=0, so Z=2 sum_{s_j=+1} p_j takes only -16,-12,...,16. Hence p9(Z) annihilates every C-neutral vector, not only S. This is a finite-algebra deduction, not a new formal theorem. For rank-one X of norm r, scale roots by r; for r=0 Z=0. Extending the seed spectral weights to all unit rank-one directions uses actual connected spin/color covariance and singlet invariance, which must be source-matched if promoted to a theorem. The exact prior nine-dimensional seed computation shows all nine roots occur at its fixture. Do not infer rank-nine for all states or for rank greater than one. The full operator on non-neutral vectors may have roots 2 mod 4.

## Exact derivative formula at source coordinate fixture

At X[2,2]=1 in zero-based coordinates, write Z=sum P_j with signs incorporated into P_j (different convention from the preceding diagonal gauge argument). The literal source signed monomial gamma yields sixteen disjoint Majorana pairs. For a different color coordinate B=dZ/dX[a,r] (a!=2), each source bilinear summand B_i shares exactly one Majorana with exactly one P_{j(i)}, commutes with the other fifteen, and anticommutes with that one. Therefore Z B_i = B_i (Z-2 P_{j(i)}). Since P_j^2=I and [P_j,Z]=0, exact finite product-rule differentiation gives
D_n=sum_{k=0}^{n-1} Z^k B Z^{n-1-k}
=sum_i B_i sum_{h=0}^{n-1} binom(n,h+1)(-2 P_{j(i)})^h Z^{n-1-h}.
Define C_B=sum_i B_i P_{j(i)}. Then D_n S is the sum over even h of binom(n,h+1)(-2)^h B Z^{n-1-h} S and over odd h of the same coefficient times C_B Z^{n-1-h} S. For all n, the transverse derivative images are contained in B V + C_B V, dimension <=18 at this seed fixture by the previously exact rank-nine recurrence. This is containment, not a measured rank or invariance under arbitrary operators. Formula holds before selecting S; no commutativity assumption between B and Z. The pre-existing rank-one orbit audit already covers the full reduced witness derivative contractions; do not count these new comparisons as previously missing coverage. This is a new compression identity, not a fresh reconstruction of Phi/H3 or higher-rank jets.
