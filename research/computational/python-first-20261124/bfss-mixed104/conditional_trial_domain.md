# Conditional cutoff lemma for the fixed-regulator witness

Status: analytic research note, not native Lean qualification. The missing hypothesis is the typed source-carrier and gauge-covariance identification. Reuses RAD-C3's original weighted graph membership characterization (strict run #43570463487), not an assertion that every vector in the unweighted domain is in the weighted domain.

Let X be a 3x9 real configuration, r=||X||, and w=(1+r^2)^(1/2). For each positive pair (a,b) write A_n=aI-(a-b)nn^T, n in the unit color sphere. The projected Gaussian is an orientation average of exp(-tr(A_n XX^T)/2), with a fixed scalar normalization. With m=min(a,b)>0, every A_n is bounded and A_n>=mI. Differentiation of any finite order under the compact sphere integral is justified by a uniform polynomial-times-Gaussian bound. In particular the profile and its gradient satisfy |f|<=C exp(-mr^2/2), |grad f|<=C(1+r) exp(-mr^2/2). If P is any finite Fock-valued polynomial of degree d, then fP and its first derivatives are bounded by C(1+r)^(d+1) exp(-mr^2/2). For this witness d<=8 and m=29/20. These claims hold for finite linear combinations and include the profile derivative, not only the polynomial derivative.

Assume the finite Fock data are identified with the literal original fiber, the differential charge expression agrees on the transported functions, and the transported scalar-plus-covariant combinations are gauge invariant under the literal original action. Then the trial psi has Q_alpha psi bounded in norm by C(1+r)^(d+2) exp(-mr^2/2), since each original charge has constant first-order coefficients and quadratic multiplication coefficients. Thus psi, each Q_alpha psi, and w Q_alpha psi lie in the appropriate L2 spaces.

Choose a smooth scalar chi on [0,infinity) with chi=1 on [0,1], chi=0 on [4,infinity), and bounded derivative, and define chi_R(X)=chi(r^2/R^2) for R>=1. It is gauge invariant and Spin(9)-invariant, so psi_R=chi_R psi belongs to the actual compactly supported invariant smooth core under the stated identification. Its derivative has norm at most 4||chi'||_infinity/R and is supported on R<=r<=2R. The product rule gives

Q_alpha(psi_R)-Q_alpha(psi)=(chi_R-1)Q_alpha(psi)-i sum_mu C^alpha_mu (partial_mu chi_R) psi.

The first term converges to zero even in weighted L2 by dominated convergence. On the derivative support w/R<=sqrt(5), so the weighted error of the second term is bounded by a constant times ||1_{r>=R} psi||_2, which tends to zero. The same argument gives ordinary L2 and unweighted charge convergence. Consequently the original minimal closed physical charge graph contains (psi,Q psi). The RAD-C3 inverse-image graph condition with reciprocal multiplier B_1=w^-1 gives (psi,w Q psi) in the weighted graph. Normalization is harmless if the mass is strictly positive.

This closes the analytic cutoff mechanism conditionally on source identification. It does not bind the numerical occupation map to the original typed carrier, establish native global gauge/Spin(9) singlet correspondence, prove the auxiliary Hardy/fidelity bound on the original closed domain, or assert a kernel vector. No Lean source changed.
