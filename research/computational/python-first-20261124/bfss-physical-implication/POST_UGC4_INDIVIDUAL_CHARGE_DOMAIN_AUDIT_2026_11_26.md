Both v_e->u_R and D v_e->D u_R in L2. All supports for 0<e<=1 lie in one compact set, so V v_e->V u_R in L2 by boundedness there. Thus Q v_e->Q u_R, proving (u_R,Q u_R) lies in the graph closure.
