# GM5E10 R1 fixed-ball zero-mode criterion

23 November 2026 · Unified integration · Candidate for native and independent strict verification

Let \(f_n\) be an original gauge-invariant smooth-core sequence with \(\|f_n\|_2\le 1\) and \(Tf_n\to0\) in the native closed sixteen-charge output space. Fix a closed ball of radius \(R\) and suppose the squared norms integrated over that ball stay above \(m>0\). Supply a finite smooth real gauge-invariant IMS partition \(\sum_j\chi_j^2=1\), a common squared gradient bound \(D\), and a member \(\chi_{j_0}\) which is one on the ball and zero outside radius \(S\). The conclusion is a nonzero physical zero mode on the natural domain of the original normalized Hamiltonian.

For \(g_n=\chi_{j_0} f_n\), the ball indicator gives \(m\le\|g_n\|_2^2\le1\) and the support condition gives \(\|\sqrt{\|X\|}\,g_n\|_2^2\le S\). The [qualified native IMS identity](BFSS_GM5E7_R2_ORIGINAL_GAUGE_CORE_LOCALIZATION_GREEN_ACCEPTANCE_2026_11_23.md) and nonnegativity of the other terms give
\[
\|Tg_n\|^2 \le \|Tf_n\|^2+8\,(\#\{j\})D\,\|f_n\|_2^2.
\]
Convergence of the original charge sequence gives a uniform bound on its range. These are the three upper bounds and the positive retained norm required by the [qualified GM5E9 criterion](BFSS_GM5E9_R1_ORIGINAL_CUTOFF_ZERO_MODE_GREEN_ACCEPTANCE_2026_11_23.md). The independent consumer uses the literal paired SU(2) charge column, closed-ball probability integral, and original natural Hamiltonian domain.

The fixed partition is an explicit hypothesis of this gate. The radial existence step can use a smooth bump equal to one inside \(R\) and zero outside \(2R\), with its value converted to an angle. Cosine and sine of that angle give a square partition and the gradients vanish outside the transition region. An actual vanishing-charge trial sequence retaining positive mass in one ball is a separate open problem. The moving-annulus negative control does not satisfy the mass hypothesis.
