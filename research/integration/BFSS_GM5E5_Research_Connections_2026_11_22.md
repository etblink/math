## What joins up

GM5E5 connects three pieces of research that previously stopped at different interfaces. The FCP work fixed the original massless smooth-core energy and constructed the gauge-physical closed sixteen-charge graph. The November weighted Sobolev work showed how control of the gradient and first radial moment gives compactness. GM5A–GM5E4 supplied the missing estimate from original gauge-invariant core data to that Sobolev input. GM5E5 applies the older theorem to the joint limits on the original 27-coordinate Fock-vector \(L^2\) carrier. The November compactness source was reused with its previously tested source bytes. [FCP-to-unified chronology](https://github.com/etblink/math/blob/42db5bd85f43f7471331fd690fe008539ef290a9/contributions/bfss-kotler/internal-monograph/HISTORICAL_LINEAGE.md) · [Compactness donor #42320696105](https://github.com/etblink/math/actions/runs/42320696105) · [GM5E5 R2 #45128649701](https://github.com/etblink/math/actions/runs/45128649701)

Write \(T\) for the original native closed charge column, \(r(X)=\|X\|\) for the global radius, and \(Wu=\sqrt r\,u\). The native Hamiltonian is \(H=T^\dagger T/16\) on its natural operator domain. Its closed quadratic form is \(\|Tu\|^2/16\) on \(D(T)\). GM5E5 takes joint strong convergence of the core input, charge output, and weighted input for each sequence member. With uniform bounds on the three limits, GM5E4 controls the gradient by \(\|\nabla u_j\|^2\le \|q_j\|^2/8+2C_M\|w_j\|^2\), where \(C_M\) is the original fermion growth constant. The compactness theorem then supplies a strongly convergent subsequence of the inputs. The first moment is supplied as a hypothesis. Extending the result to all of \(D(T)\cap D(W)\) calls for a separate joint-core theorem.
