# Truth-first foundational research charter

**Project Lead mandate reaffirmed 2026-11-18.** The objective of this research program is to discover which mathematical and physical claims are true, which are false or unsupported, and what can be tested against reality. **Proving BFSS valid is not the goal.** BFSS is a research subject and potential source of reusable mathematical insights, not a favored conclusion or a substitute for evidence. The same applies to Nested Fibrational Cosmology, strings, loops, category-theoretic proposals, and all competing approaches.

## Scientific hierarchy

1. **Observed reality and rigorously reported experimental constraints** govern claims about nature. Standard Model phenomenology, neutrino observations, cosmology, gravitation, and established precision experiments must be addressed on their own terms. The Standard Model is a central empirical benchmark, not a task postponed until BFSS completion.
2. **Mathematical correctness** is necessary for a proposed mathematical implication, but does not entail physical applicability. A Lean GREEN establishes only the checked statement, on its literal source types under its exact hypotheses and axioms. Independent consumers, frozen source bytes and negative controls protect this distinction.
3. **Cross-framework comparison** must be symmetric and adversarial. Identify where a framework succeeds, fails, depends on unverified assumptions, or leaves the observed particle content/quantitative predictions unexplained. Record negative and inconclusive findings as first-class assets.
4. **Proof obligations and physical inferences remain separate.** E0/E1/E2 original Source48 action lemmas cannot by themselves imply a four-dimensional chiral (SU(3)_C\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y) theory, generation count, anomaly cancellation, mass spectrum, physical ground state, BFSS spectral gap or M-theory correctness.
5. **No theorem-protection behavior.** Do not privilege BFSS CI throughput over a higher-value competing framework test, data constraint or falsifying result. Redirect effort toward the most discriminating question, not the most comfortable proof path.

## Immediate parallel work allocation

**Start Standard Model Foundations (SM0) now as a read-only comparative investigation.** Do not make it conditional on finishing E2/E3, M4 or BFSS ground-state questions. Track, separately from BFSS mathematical gate acceptance:

- Empirical Standard Model content: actual (SU(3)_C\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y) gauge representation assignments, three chiral generations, anomaly cancellation, spontaneous electroweak symmetry breaking, Higgs/Yukawa sector, masses and mixings, observed neutrino effects, tested precision constraints and known limitations.
- What the original finite (SU(2)) BFSS model **does not automatically explain**: equality of SU(2) labels across different physical contexts, a four-dimensional Standard Model gauge group, chirality, three generations, hypercharge assignments, neutrino phenomenology, gravity, or a Standard Model particle spectrum. The 48 source Majoranas are not evidence for three Standard Model generations.
- Compare BFSS, established relativistic QFT, geometry, algebraic QFT, and other plausible structures using **exact typed maps or explicit obstructions**, never visual numerology.
- Produce an SM0 requirements-to-evidence matrix with columns: empirical proposition; verified observation/source; necessary mathematical structure; exact existing qualified Lean interface (if any); gap/no-go; possible discriminating calculation or experiment; confidence/status. A proposal is not a result.
- SM1 may propose a separate bounded formal gate only after a concrete target is stated. Treat alternative methods fairly, and prioritize by relevance, uncertainty reduction and potential falsifiability, not by which framework owns a repository.

## Evidence and operational discipline

- One canonical record, immutable original native RED/GREEN evidence, tested commit, dependency pins, full job log, original ZIP hashes/CRC, individual source and independent consumer axiom reports.
- A harness RED, source-native RED, consumer-native RED and physical counterexample are **different**. Do not misstate them. Two analogous native proof REDs impose an architecture stop; do not simply increase Lean recursion or repeat the same source expansion.
- Keep BFSS work on `integration/bfss-unified-research-20261108` unless the owner changes the instruction; do not modify `main`; preserve canonical `BFSS_GATE_STATE.json` and historical accepted anchors unless their exact qualification criteria are met.
- Gate status and mathematical claims must remain conditional on verified CI and independent archival. An archived source-only GREEN after a failed consumer remains **unqualified** as an integrated proof.
- Revisit comparative research priorities at milestone transitions and whenever the owner calls out our foundational motivation. Transparently distinguish *what we have proved*, *what is physically supported*, and *what we are exploring*.

**North star:** mathematical rigor serves empirical inquiry; neither the proof program nor any favored candidate theory is the reason for the research. We seek a more accurate account of physical reality, even when the answer invalidates an earlier hypothesis.
