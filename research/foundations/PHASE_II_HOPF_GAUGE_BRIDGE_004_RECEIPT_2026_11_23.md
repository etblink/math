## Negative result: distinguish Clifford matrix grade from Fock operator degree
For normalized Hermitian Majoranas {gamma_i,gamma_j}=2 delta_ij I, any real symmetric M obeys sum_ij M_ij gamma_i gamma_j = Tr(M) I, by symmetry and the CAR. Color contraction over three copies gives 3 Tr(M) I. Thus a traceless symmetric grade-four Clifford matrix in the 16-dimensional spinor representation cannot be faithfully lifted by that naive bilinear contraction; this is an analytic identity, not a numerical inference. This does not rule out other covariant operators involving references, higher products or different coefficient types. In particular an antisymmetric coefficient can give a nontrivial bivector; source LB1C1 handles this distinction.
