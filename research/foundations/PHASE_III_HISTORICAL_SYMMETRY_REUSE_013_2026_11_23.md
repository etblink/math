## Analytic alternative to compare: lift the existing star action through SU(d)
This is standard finite matrix algebra/topology, an analytic proposal, not a native Lean theorem or a claim that it has not appeared before. Let F be actual finite complex Fock Hilbert space, d=2^24, and alpha the existing B3 map into Aut_*(End_C F). Under a source-compatible continuous simply-connected Spin9 topology, first establish continuity of alpha in finite-dimensional linear-map topology. If continuous original 48-real coefficient action is available, this follows from its exact all-vector Majorana covariance: each fixed word in generators transforms to a product of finite linear combinations with continuous coefficients; choose finitely many spanning words using the original Clifford-Fock AlgEquiv, then extend by linearity in a fixed basis. No exhaustive expansion into all d^2 matrix entries is mathematically needed. Continuity of the coefficient representation is a separate source obligation; identity chart statements alone do not automatically install the needed global group topology.
For a star automorphism beta of End(F), choose orthonormal basis e_j, matrix units E_jk. beta(E_00) is a rank-one orthogonal projection. For any unit v in its image, vectors v_j=beta(E_j0)v form an orthonormal basis by matrix-unit relations and adjoints. U e_j=v_j is unitary, beta(T)=U T U^*. Near identity beta, take v=beta(E_00)e_0/||beta(E_00)e_0||, giving a continuous local section; translate to neighborhoods of arbitrary beta. Scalar unitaries are precisely the kernel by irreducibility. Multiplying U by a locally continuous d-th root of det(U)^(-1) gives SU(d) implementer. Therefore SU(d) -> Aut_*(End F) is a covering with finite kernel mu_d: local sections and distinct finite scalar translates give evenly covered neighborhoods. This avoids needing a separately source-bound Spin48 covering and even Clifford physical-star compatibility, though may be harder in Lean at the large matrix dimension. It is not enough to choose unrelated determinant roots at each group element.
Lift alpha through this covering using simple connectedness of source Spin9, fixing U(1)=I. The two maps (g,h) -> U(gh) and U(g)U(h) lift the same base map; uniqueness of lifts on connected product yields equality. Thus coherent continuous vector action conditional on source topology/continuity. Gauge compatibility can use scalar commutant and genuine SU2 character triviality from 005, or existing S1H uniqueness once exact covariance matched. Do not confuse unitary lift with determinant-normalized scalar ambiguity removed pointwise; global continuity and connectedness essential.
Central element: exact source CS0 implies implementer conjugates all normalized Majoranas to negatives; parity has same conjugation. Hence U(-1)=z P, z=+/-1 by order two. Scalar implementer impossible. This argument does not fix the actual parity sign. Source convention and connected lift may determine it via a plane path; retain this explicit check if implementation pursued. It is not necessary to assume every K(A) bracket as an input to the matrix-automorphism covering route.
