## SCC discrimination and new bounded witness
AB parity and spinor center sign share a Z2-valued comparison at selected settings, but differ in source and mechanism. Neither alone identifies complete underlying transport algebra. Unpolarized spin measurement with a fixed reference gives Re Tr(U)/2 = cos(theta/2), blind to rotation axis. Full SU(2) reconstruction not established by that readout.
Exact new *model* test (not an experimental report): A=-i X, B=-i Z are spinor pi rotations about orthogonal axes. Both have trace zero, yet AB=-BA, ABA^-1 B^-1=-I. Direct coherent comparison of the two opposite orders has relative operator (AB)^† BA=-I, unlike commuting scalar U(1) phases whose opposite orders coincide when individual operations are fixed. Script verifies algebra. A polarized preparation rho=(I+Y)/2 and quadrature reference can also distinguish A and B in a direct two-arm comparison; an ordinary unpolarized fixed-reference trace cannot. Need independently calibrated same operations, controlled order/path, coherence, dynamical phase controls and physical feasibility. Apparatus-dependent scalar phase or changed controls can mimic the order difference; not an unconditional experimental exclusion of all abelian models. Standard quantum spin predicts it, so it is not a new discriminating prediction of H1 versus QM.
