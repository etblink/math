## Hurwitz boundary
The classification of finite-dimensional real unital algebras with bilinear multiplication and positive-definite multiplicative quadratic norm gives R,C,H,O. These hypotheses are substantial; norm-preserving transformations acting on a vector space do not imply a multiplicative norm on the algebra of transformations or a division algebra. E.g. O(2) acts by norm-preserving matrices, but its real linear span is M2(R), containing two nonzero complementary projections with zero product, checked exactly. Under associativity only R,C,H remain.
