## Motivation and method
The aim is truth about nature, not confirmation of NFC, BFSS, IBI or any favored model. Observe, question, research, hypothesize, test, draw conclusions, report. Record both positive and negative evidence. Do not call an aspiration a well-posed hypothesis. "Hypothesis of Everything" is a research aspiration, not a claimed result. Conventional GUT concerns the strong, weak and electromagnetic interactions; the broader objective also includes gravity and spacetime.

Separate (E) empirical observations with measured scope and uncertainty; (M) mathematical theorem with assumptions and exact evidence; (N) negative result with domain and counterexample; (H) conjecture with explicit falsifier; (B) physical bridge with correspondence and independent validation. A green proof does not supply an empirical witness. A witness of existence does not supply uniqueness, and reconstruction up to equivalence does not establish ontological priority.

## Incidence before Identity (IBI)
Methodological IBI: do not assume independently labeled objects or additional structure when the relevant identity can be reconstructed from specified incidence and compatibility data, modulo the physically appropriate equivalence. Mathematical identity is not abolished; rather, primitive physical individuation is not presupposed in a reconstruction inquiry. Original IBI motivates minimal relational structure, constrained composition, and internal distinguishability; do not silently introduce background space, time, labels or observers as derived facts. Physical IBI is a stronger, unproved claim that the physically meaningful identities and dynamics of nature can be reconstructed from relational data with no independent identity-bearing primitives. Keep these meanings separate. Relations themselves need a specified mathematical carrier and interpretation.

## First bounded hypothesis (IBI-R1; proposed, not claimed qualified)
For X,Y real 3-by-9 bosonic color-column arrays with the SO(3) left action, define G_ij = x_i dot x_j and D_ijk = det[x_i,x_j,x_k]. Hypothesis: equality of all G and D is equivalent to Y=RX for some R in SO(3). Test every rank 0,1,2,3. Gram equality gives an orthogonal isometry on the spans; at rank less than three an extension's determinant can be corrected on the orthogonal complement; at rank three the sign of a nonzero triple fixes the determinant. This is a standard finite-dimensional mathematical argument to be checked and bound to the original source; do not mark a Lean converse qualified without a new or recovered exact receipt. The already qualified adjoint surjection offers a conditional route to the native SU(2) bosonic orbit statement once source action and equivalence are matched. Reflection of full-rank data is an explicit Gram-only negative control; rank-deficient data must not be falsely treated as oriented full-rank data. Counterexample criterion: any same-data pair not related by the required action, or any required source-binding failure. No claim of reconstruction of Fock degrees of freedom or of physical ontology.
