Since gamma_ji=Gamma_j Gamma_i=-Gamma_i Gamma_j for i!=j and gamma_jj=0, the four product terms reduce to minus twice the LHS of III009. The last two terms are -2||v||² Gamma_j[s,r]+2 delta_rs(v^T Gamma_j v). Divide by -2 and use gamma symmetry: LHS=-||v||² Gamma_j+(v^T Gamma_j v)I. No change of dimension, sign convention or Grassmann spinor contraction assumed beyond the stated commuting v.
