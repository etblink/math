## Shared assumptions and discipline
Carrier and mathematical equality, incidence, composability, test admissibility, dynamics, finite enumeration, comparison and in some probes synchronized steps, probability, real/complex amplitudes, Born rule and standard tensor composition are introduced, not derived. The analyst's mathematical access is not a physical observer. A test-relative quotient is not automatically a physical ontology. Finiteness of witnesses is not ontological finiteness. No privileged unlimited global test; no primitive conscious observer. Separate structural role, species/type, state, and re-identification of a continuing individual. Distinguish gauge redundancy from inaccessible information. Gauge-frame invariance is not necessarily physical dynamical stability.

## H1 — bounded candidate, not a universal theorem
For a specified physical sector, seek a *predeclared*, finitely implementable relational witness family W and independently specified physical equivalence E. Test whether equality of W characterizes E for that sector, whether transport and composition descend appropriately, and whether selected distinctions survive independently constrained perturbations. A successful test supports only that sector. Do not define E retrospectively as equality of W or define admissible interactions solely to preserve W: that would make reconstruction circular. Counterexamples: same W but inequivalent states with a licensed distinguishing context, different W for gauge-equivalent states, failure of compositional substitution, non-realizable witness, or independently admissible evolution that destroys a claimed protected distinction. Distinguish empirical prediction and model reconstruction; no assertion that all identity reduces to relations. Compare object-first and joint-emergence alternatives if operationally equivalent. A program of identity reconstruction is not yet a falsifiable Hypothesis of Everything.
