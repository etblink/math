## 2. Exact target and coefficient reduction

Let G_i integer lift, B_ij=G_i G_j. Define ordered integer coefficient residual

 sum_(i!=j)[G_i[a,p] B_ij[b,q]+ B_ij[a,p] G_i[b,q]]

The former 136 basis/pair-sum probes determine the same polynomial; the coefficient proof is a more direct transfer.

## 3. Independent structural proof route (analytic, not Lean checked)

Distinct words multiply (up to sign) to a nonempty Clifford word of length <=8. Its trace is zero: for even length choose a generator in its support, for odd length choose outside it; conjugation reverses sign, trace invariant. Thus orthogonal and independent, and 1+9+C(9,4)=136=16*17/2. This proof uses only real symmetric Clifford relations and matrix dimension. Python checked the actual 136-word Gram matrix equals 16I.

The eight remaining axes contain four in its support and four outside, whose conjugations give + and - respectively, so cancel. Hence equality for all symmetric M, including vv^T.
