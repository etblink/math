## Implication for IBI / NFC
Define local equivalence of same-type processes by equality of every allowed isolated preparation/readout probability. Define contextual equivalence by equality of outcome probabilities in every admissible well-typed circuit containing the process in a specified hole, including auxiliary systems and joint effects. If these contexts are closed under substitution/composition, contextual equivalence is a congruence: any proposed replacement inside a larger context is itself one of the tests in the definition. It is the coarsest equivalence consistent with those contextual probabilities (all identical signatures grouped). This is a conditional operational definition and elementary reasoning, not a derivation of admissible contexts or empirical ontology. Local equivalence is not necessarily a congruence under tensor extension: C_+1 and C_-1 witness failure. Avoid quotienting away hidden degrees before checking compositional compatibility.
