## 2. What is physically missing, in dependency order

A comparable candidate prediction requires an independently source-derived, convention- and unit-correct chain:

1. **Physical sector**: a controlled 3+1-dimensional low-energy theory with identified neutrino and charged-lepton states, chiral electroweak representations, relevant quantum numbers and interactions. A 0+1-dimensional SU(2) BFSS gauge symmetry is *not* automatically the Standard Model's SU(2)_L; the `Spin(9)` 16-component representation is *not* the observed PMNS mixing matrix.
2. **Mass mechanism**: the candidate's explicit, justified neutrino mass term/effective operator and any right-handed/seesaw fields, along with a separately sourced charged-lepton mass operator, scale, parameters and flavor basis. If the mass spectrum is postulated or calibrated from JUNO data, mark it a **fit input**.
3. **Diagonalization and labeling**: derived mass eigenvalues `m1²,m2²,m3²` in `eV²`, ordering and label conventions, and mixing matrix `U_PMNS = U_e† U_ν` from consistently diagonalized source matrices. Do not assume a 3×3 Hermitian toy operator is a correctly normalized Dirac or Majorana neutrino mass term.
4. **Source-independent number**: an explicit prediction `Δm²21=m2²−m1²` whose input parameters were fixed **without** using that same measured splitting; track any admissible parameter family and theory error. If this arrow fails, stop before an observational compatibility score.
5. **Oscillation to detector**: derived `P(ν̅_e→ν̅_e;E,L)`, production/flavor states, reactor flux and nuisance inputs, JUNO energy response and a source-authored likelihood/covariance; keep normal/inverted ordering cases and external Daya Bay/accelerator inputs distinct.
6. **Evidence**: a preregistered or otherwise independently checkable comparison against **versioned** measurements and an explicit negative/null control. No Gaussian likelihood, two-parameter independence or significance may be invented from a ± label.

For BFSS the first arrow remains unqualified, so later arrows are not established. For frozen NFC, conditional structural matter/Higgs claims do not supply arrows 2–5, and the canon explicitly declines PMNS and particle-mass claims. For the measured neutrino-extended SM parameterization, mass splittings and mixing can be empirically **fitted inputs**; that is a valid model of data but is not automatically a parameter-free prediction.

**Stop rule**: if no independently derived, source-bound physical mass and mixing map exists, preserve `MISSING_PHYSICAL_MAP` and a list of exact unresolved arrows. Do not manufacture model values or add abstract proof gates to simulate empirical progress.
