| SCC-style element | SM-D1 translation | Must be proved or checked |

| Admissible counterfactual | A specified change of hypothesis or model parameter, with fixed experiment protocol | Not a physical laboratory intervention unless actually performed |

The central research problem is **observational distinguishability**, not assigning a scalar "capacity" score to a model.

Fix an explicitly specified model family M, admissible changes c, and a predeclared set W of observables. If two variants have genuine predictive distributions p and q under the same experimental protocol, an exact theoretical separation witness exists when

\[
\exists w\in W:\quad p_M(y_w\mid\theta,c)\ne p_N(y_w\mid\phi,d).
\]

A missing prediction produces UNKNOWN/NOT_PREDICTED, **not** equality or agreement. Different distributions may still be practically indistinguishable given finite data, noise, correlated errors and nuisance parameter re-fitting. Conversely, two models with equal observable predictions on W are observationally equivalent **only relative to W**, not necessarily physically or ontologically identical.

A minimal distinguishing family W* would separate specified admissible model pairs while removal of any witness destroys some required distinction. Such a family need not be unique or exist under currently available measurements. This is a **new proposed specification**, not an assertion that the earlier SCC MCS theorem has been established or specializes here.

## Pilot 2: neutrino masses and insufficient witnesses

SM-D0 includes JUNO measurements of the solar mixing angle and mass-squared splitting, obtained in one analysis and not statistically independent. Standard neutrino-oscillation probabilities depend on mass-squared **differences**, not a common absolute mass-squared offset. Therefore, under controlled admissible mass-spectrum variations preserving splittings and mixing, oscillation witnesses alone do not determine the overall mass baseline.

SCC-style witness sufficiency asks: **which additional physically justified observable would break that degeneracy?** Potential tests include direct beta-decay mass sensitivity or other absolute-mass probes, with separately sourced values, assumptions, uncertainties and covariance. No mass value, exclusion strength or unique model mechanism is asserted here.

**Conclusion:** this is a useful identifiability question, not an already-qualified neutrino theorem in our repository.
