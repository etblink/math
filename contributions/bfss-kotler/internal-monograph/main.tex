This is a \emph{living internal mathematical research monograph}, intended as an
onboarding and research instrument for mathematicians and mathematical physicists.
This edition contains substantive mathematical narratives of the original
carriers and Hamiltonian (Chapters 2--6) and the source Spin(9) geometry,
Fock-operator algebra and gauge-covariance frontier (Chapters 7--9).
Chapters 1 and 10--14 remain architectural outlines. Formal records document historical and current
qualification without claiming new Lean proofs. The core-source research snapshot is
\texttt{etblink/math} at commit \texttt{d4870eb1ac58}; the monograph editorial
branch is independently versioned.

The subject is the source-exact relative $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ supersymmetric matrix
quantum-mechanical system. The name BFSS identifies the equations' lineage;
identification with eleven-dimensional M-theory is \emph{not} assumed. There are
nine spatial labels, three color labels, sixteen spinor indices, and forty-eight
real Majorana labels. In particular, the 27 real bosonic configuration
coordinates are \emph{not} 27 spacetime dimensions.

Throughout the completed manuscript, a result must be identified as one of:
(i) an original source definition, (ii) historically strict-qualified,
(iii) strict-qualified in the integration repository, (iv) source present with
receipt pending, (v) a pending formal candidate, (vi) conditional analytical
argument, or (vii) open proof obligation. Neither compiler success alone nor
a conditional derivation supplies an unstated physical theorem.
