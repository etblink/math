\section{Purpose of the chronological record}
The mathematical chapters are deliberately ordered by logical dependence,
not by the date a proof was found. But historical order matters to an
independent researcher: an October note describing a theorem as unproved
may have been superseded by a strict-green result two days later. The
chronology below identifies \emph{which question was answered when}, the
exact source repository, and the boundary that remained after each step.
No historical audit is treated as stronger than its pinned theorem statement.

\section{Chronological recovery of the energy and compactness argument}
A separate analytical lineage is easy to miss when reading the November
Spin(9) and Clifford work first. In October, FCP G4--K9D established the
literal source undeformed potential/charge/core-form comparison at
$(h,m)=(1,0)$, with strict
\href{https://github.com/etblink/Foundational-Convergence-Program/actions/runs/38011995220}{\#38011995220}.
The same October program proved an exact complete graph model of the
physical closed charge form through D2B23--D2B25, then identified the
native self-adjoint physical Hamiltonian through BA--BH. These form-graph
results concern the actual charge operator and its natural domain.

The complementary November 6 source program was strict-qualified on
7 November UTC. First-moment cutoff/tightness, smooth-core gradient
Rellich compactness, weighted-closure compactness and the actual original
smooth-core massless energy composition were accepted, respectively, at
\href{https://github.com/etblink/math/actions/runs/42315568670}{\#42315568670},
\href{https://github.com/etblink/math/actions/runs/42318037096}{\#42318037096},
\href{https://github.com/etblink/math/actions/runs/42320696105}{\#42320696105}, and
\href{https://github.com/etblink/math/actions/runs/42325087575}{\#42325087575}.
The sequence is not redundant: the first three certify an
\emph{abstract implication} from already bounded gradients and radial
moments, whereas the fourth certifies a \emph{source-generic exact energy
identity}. Neither derives the actual physical high-Spin coercivity
estimate that could connect them.

The older spectral A/B source audit's finite-CAR constant $8\sqrt3$ and
source color-wedge diagnostics remain analytical and computational,
not an exact certified original fermionic norm theorem. Even a fully
proved linear-growth constant would leave the original rank-one flat
valleys, correct source Spin(9) reducing sectors, and form-domain
identification to be addressed. Chapters~\ref{ch:energy} and
\ref{ch:compactness} reconstruct these boundaries in mathematical order.

\section{How a future researcher should use the record}
Begin with a named question and its mathematical carrier. Read the
current formal statement, then the most recent source audit and
independent strict-green receipt; read older handoffs to recover
\emph{why} the proof was needed and why previous approaches failed.
Never use the date of a handoff to decide whether a theorem is still
open. Never infer current integration acceptance from a historical
FCP green when the imported proof or its dependency closure has
changed. Above all, distinguish a genuine source theorem, a
conditional analytic reduction, and the still-open spectral conclusion.
