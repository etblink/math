\section{Why this appendix belongs in an internal research monograph}

For an independent collaborator, a list of successful Lean declarations
is not enough. A rejected proof route may be the most economical way
to prevent a mistaken future construction. We therefore record
negative findings with their \emph{minimal mathematical scope},
separating a verified counterexample, a conditional objection, and
an implementation failure. The full chronology and exact SHA-based
receipts remain in \texttt{HISTORICAL\_LINEAGE.md}, the research
memoranda, and the theorem atlas; this appendix is a reader's map.

\section{Mathematical and representation-theoretic stop conditions}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,style=nextline]
 \item[Source central sign cannot be discarded.]
 The original $z=-1\in\operatorname{Spin}(9)$ is trivial on
 the nine-vector representation but acts as $-I$ on the original
 48 Majorana label carrier. Sending $z$ to a scalar operator by a
 simple unital $\mathrm{Cl}_9$-algebra homomorphism would yield
 trivial conjugation and cannot implement the required CAR action.
 This excludes that \emph{particular shortcut}, not all continuous
 group-lift constructions.
 \item[Commuting source label matrices do not prove commuting Fock unitaries.]
 The accepted C48 source matrices on the forty-eight labels commute.
 On a finite Fock representation, operators implementing commuting
 automorphisms can differ by a scalar phase. Gauge-unitary
 fixedness, a globally consistent implementing representation, and
 the original physical projection still require their own proofs.
 \item[Fock-vector and endomorphism fibers carry different weights.]
 A possible bound $6$ for the original Fock-vector first weight is
 not a bound for the full endomorphism fiber. The latter has the
 contragredient input index and reaches first weight $12$. In the
 conditional oscillator argument the operator-valued threshold is
 $\lambda_2>m+12$. Source binding must precede a numerical
 confinement claim.
 \item[Rank-one valleys prohibit naive radial bosonic confinement.]
 The quartic commutator potential vanishes on commuting rank-one
 configurations. An unbounded potential away from these valleys is
 insufficient to prove compact resolvent of the full Hamiltonian.
 An actual escaping sequence in a \emph{fixed physical sector} would
 be a substantive adverse test; merely pointing to one classical
 commuting configuration is not such a sequence.
 \item[Color multiplicity is not generation multiplicity.]
 The three SU(2) adjoint-color directions mix under the physical
 gauge group. Three independent rank-one color projectors cannot
 all commute with this irreducible real action. This rules out the
 naive direct color-as-three-family identification, not every
 possible emergent gauge-invariant composite construction.
 \item[Hopf bundles must pass gauge descent.]
 The source nine-gamma eight-plus-eight eigenspace projectors are
 interchanged under antipodal spatial direction. A proposed
 individually named plus-bundle on the unoriented rank-one valley
 quotient requires an explicit gauge-equivariant descent map.
 Existing topological and category-theoretic objections are
 conditional source-bridge arguments, not a formalized no-go theorem
 against all physical or stacky structures.
\end{description}

\section{Implementation failures are not automatically counterexamples}

Some failures in the historical project had precise nonmathematical
causes: a generated manifest syntax error, a diagnostic file-path
mismatch, an API deprecation treated as a warning-as-error, a Lean
style-linter warning, or a proof elaborator issue in a large monolithic
construction. They should be preserved because they informed more
reliable bounded methods, but no failed job should be cited as a
counterexample without a mathematical refutation.

The operational response is to locate the \emph{first causal error},
compare the candidate against the last qualified source and consumer,
repair only the smallest necessary surface, and freeze the theorem
statement and mathematical normalization. A cold source build,
independent consumer, exact dependency pins, and allowed-axiom
reports supply a reproducible certificate when they all pass.

\section{Historical qualification is not current-tree acceptance}

The project spans the FCP research corpus, the original upstream
mathematical source, multiple research branches, and the unified
\texttt{etblink/math} integration branch. A theorem's existence in
that tree is a source-location fact. A historical strict-green
qualification belongs to the exact tested Git object and dependencies.
A present integration certificate is a separate statement requiring
its own tested source closure. The manuscript's \emph{formalization
records} link these layers but are not independent proof certificates.

The manuscript itself is built locally from its \LaTeX{} sources.
Generated PDF files, auxiliary compiler files and visual render
images are review products, not components of the source-only Git
commit. GitHub Actions, Pages, webhooks and hosted compilation are
not prerequisites for compiling or reviewing the book.

\section{A repeatable reader audit}

For a theorem to be promoted from conditional research to an accepted
result in the final external monograph, require at least the following:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
 \item A complete conventional theorem statement, including exact
 source types and hypotheses, with a proof or an explicit reduction
 to previously accepted lemmas.
 \item Exact Lean declaration, source blob and tested dependency
 closure; no substitution of an equivalent but unbound physical model.
 \item Independently compiled consumer and a closed, fail-fast
 allowed-axiom report at the tested commit.
 \item If a spectral theorem is claimed, exact physical-sector
 reduction, the original charge-form domain bridge, and the energy
 estimate on that same closed form.
 \item If a physical unification claim is advanced, a definition of
 observables and dynamics and an independently discriminating
 empirical consequence, or an honest statement that none exists.
\end{enumerate}
