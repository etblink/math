\section{What a candidate must explain}
The Standard Model work runs independently of the BFSS spectral
program. It supplies targets against which claims of emergence can
be evaluated. A candidate must give a physically defined map to the
observables it claims to predict. For particle structure this includes
the chiral representations, hypercharges, and multiplicities on the
physical degrees of freedom. For a numerical prediction it includes
the units, parameter conventions, and experimental comparison.
Chapter~\ref{ch:gut-representations} gives a concrete example of why
a label count cannot provide this map.

\section{Four kinds of evidence in the current record}
The one-family anomaly check evaluates specified anomaly coefficients
from declared chiral multiplets and charges. Its premises contain the
representation and multiplicity data. Its conclusion is the arithmetic
cancellation for those inputs.

The empirical ledger records source-attributed constraints with
uncertainties and provenance. Its checks establish the integrity of
the curated intake. Candidate-derived values, experimental covariance,
and likelihoods enter as distinct evidence when supplied.

Structural Counterfactual Capacity (SCC) asks which candidate
differences survive an observable map. In the finite setting, fix
two families of admissible inputs, a set of measurement protocols,
and predictions on their declared outcomes. A selected protocol is
a pairwise witness when it has valid predictions on both inputs and
those predictions differ. To distinguish two whole families uniformly,
the selected protocols must provide a witness for every admissible
cross-family pair. Missing predictions leave an unanswered pair.
The formal total-variation result gives an exact distinction test
for rational finite categorical distributions within its stated
premises. The executable decision tool handles completeness and
protocol validity. A joint statistical exclusion would need a
specified joint likelihood and its assumptions.

\section{A worked identifiability boundary}
The neutrino intake carries a specified solar mass-squared
difference and its source conventions. If a putative comparison
depends only on \(m_2^2-m_1^2\), then
\[
 (m_2^2+c)-(m_1^2+c)=m_2^2-m_1^2
\]
for any common shift \(c\) preserving the admissible squared masses.
The difference therefore cannot identify the common absolute offset.
This is a diagnostic of the observable map. Connecting BFSS to that
intake would require a candidate-derived map to the measured
difference with identified physical states and units. The recorded
intake contains zero qualified candidate-derived predictions.

The same discipline applies internally to the quantizer. A real
symmetric trace-free change in its coefficient is invisible to
the quadratic Fock operator by the CAR contraction. Establishing
equivalence for other interactions or a complete candidate model
would require testing their own observable maps. The
counterfactual dossier records the derivation and computational
controls as separate evidence.
